## Script — 20 beats (VO + matched shot)

**1 · 0:00 — HOOK**
- VO: "There's a question every sci-fi RPG fan has been quietly afraid to ask. After the studio shake-ups, the divisive sequels, the long silences — can the next great space-opera actually live up to the ones that defined us?"
- Shot: Lone armored figure from behind at a vast window overlooking a star-filled galaxy; slow push-in.

**2 · 0:15 — THE UNCOMFORTABLE TRUTH**
- VO: "Because here's the uncomfortable truth. Nostalgia is doing a lot of heavy lifting right now. We remember the highs so vividly we've half-forgotten how fragile they always were."
- Shot: Drifting forward through a dim derelict spaceship corridor, dust motes in shafts of light.

**3 · 0:30 — INVITATION + TITLE STING**
- VO: "I've spent years pulling apart what makes these worlds work — and what makes them fall apart. So grab a cup of tea, get comfortable, and let's talk about what the next one has to get right."
- Shot: A warm mug of tea steaming on a console, a spiral galaxy reflected in the window behind it.

**4 · 0:45 — PART 1: people > galaxy**
- VO: "Start with what the golden age understood better than anyone: a galaxy is only as big as the people in it. The ships, the planets, the wars — none of it mattered without a crew you'd die for."
- Shot: Silhouetted crew around a glowing holographic table; slow orbit.

**5 · 1:00**
- VO: "Those games made you feel the most important relationships weren't side content. They were the spine. You weren't saving the galaxy for the galaxy. You were saving it for them."
- Shot: Two figures sitting side by side on a ship's lowered ramp, watching an alien sunrise.

**6 · 1:15**
- VO: "And the real choices were never about good and evil. They were about what you were willing to lose. The best of them let a single decision haunt you twenty hours later."
- Shot: A gloved hand hovering between two glowing holographic options; slow push to the hands.

**7 · 1:30**
- VO: "That's the bar. Not the graphics. Not the scale. The feeling that this world remembered exactly who you were."
- Shot: A vast alien vista with one tiny lone figure for scale; slow crane up.

**8 · 1:45 — PART 2: where it breaks**
- VO: "So where does it go wrong? Usually the same way every time. The scope balloons — and the soul gets spread too thin."
- Shot: An overwhelming city-scale hologram dense with glowing icons; slow pull-back.

**9 · 2:00**
- VO: "When everything is epic, nothing is. A map full of icons is not a world. A hundred characters you'll never think about again is not a crew."
- Shot: An enormous empty hangar, cold and over-scaled, a single small figure crossing it.

**10 · 2:15**
- VO: "The divisive entries weren't bad because they were ambitious. They were divisive because the ambition pointed outward — at size — instead of inward, at meaning."
- Shot: A cracked helmet visor reflecting a fractured scene; slow push-in.

**11 · 2:30**
- VO: "And audiences feel that, even when they can't name it. They walk away saying it was 'fine.' And 'fine' is the most dangerous word a studio can ever hear."
- Shot: A crowd of faceless identical armored figures in fog; slow track past them.

**12 · 2:45 — PART 3: what to get right**
- VO: "So what does the next one have to do? First — be smaller, and braver. A tight cast you genuinely love beats a sprawling one you'll forget."
- Shot: Intimate two-shot, two characters in warm dim light, a hand on a shoulder; slow push-in.

**13 · 3:00**
- VO: "Second — make the choices cost something again. Not a different cutscene. A different you. The kind of decision people argue about in the comments for a decade."
- Shot: A single dramatic fork in a glowing path across a void, one figure at the split; slow tilt.

**14 · 3:15**
- VO: "Third — trust silence. The most powerful moments in these games were never the explosions. They were the quiet ones, between people, when the music dropped out."
- Shot: Two figures in silence on a quiet starship bridge, vast stars beyond; very slow drift. (Drop music here.)

**15 · 3:30**
- VO: "And fourth — respect the history without being imprisoned by it. Honour what came before, but have the nerve to become its own thing."
- Shot: A weathered monument to fallen heroes, a new lone figure gazing up; slow crane up.

**16 · 3:45**
- VO: "Get those four right, and the technology almost doesn't matter. Get them wrong, and no budget on Earth will save it."
- Shot: A sleek starship powering up, warm light spreading across its hull; slow push-in. (Music swells back.)

**17 · 4:00 — OUTLOOK**
- VO: "Am I optimistic? Cautiously. The talent is still out there, and the appetite has never been higher. But appetite is also pressure — and pressure has broken better projects than this."
- Shot: A figure on a ridge, horizon split between dark storm and breaking dawn; slow drift.

**18 · 4:15**
- VO: "The honest answer is: we don't know yet. Anyone telling you they're certain — in either direction — is selling you something."
- Shot: A glowing nebula slowly parting to half-reveal a distant structure; slow forward push.

**19 · 4:30 — CLOSE**
- VO: "What I do know is that these worlds meant something to a lot of us. And the next one deserves to be judged on whether it understands why — not just whether it's big enough."
- Shot: Wide emotional shot, a lone figure walking toward a tall glowing gateway of light; slow dolly back.

**20 · 4:45 — CTA**
- VO: "So tell me below — what's the one thing the next great sci-fi RPG has to get right for you? I read every comment, and the best ones end up in the next video. Until then — look after yourselves, keep theorising, and I'll see you in the next one."
- Shot: The galaxy window from Shot 1, the chair now empty, calm stillness; slow pull-back, gentle fade. (Subscribe end-card.)

## Production notes
- Opposite of fast-cut spectacle: let lines breathe; B-roll often a single held frame under VO.
- Structure is the engine: thesis → what's confirmed → overlooked detail → implications → realistic
  timing → invite theories. Almost any RPG topic drops into this skeleton.
- Narrator: British male, measured (Higgsfield "Alistair" preset works well).
- Music low and slow, ducked ~15% under VO; drop it out under Shot 14, swell back on Shot 16.
