# Channel Analysis — Jackdaw (@JackdawJPG / @JackdawYT)

## At a glance
"Jack," a British creator with a journalism degree, running *"the channel where modern RPGs get
lovingly overanalysed."* Began as "Mr Dragon Age Lore on YouTube," grew into the go-to source for
**news, lore, and analysis on BioWare's next games** (Dragon Age, Mass Effect) plus a widening net of
RPGs (Fable, Exodus, The Expanse: Osiris Reborn, Divinity). The name is the thesis: a jackdaw is a
corvid that hoards shiny things — Jack hoards lore and development scraps and assembles them into a
coherent picture. Signature warmth: *"brew some tea and join me."*

## Content pillars
| Pillar | What it is | Why it works |
|---|---|---|
| News breakdowns | Decode a reveal/trailer/industry story and what it *actually* means | Timely; rides each announcement's spike |
| Lore deep-dives / video essays | Long-form analysis of story, characters, worldbuilding | Evergreen, rewatchable, builds authority |
| Retrospectives | Revisit a game with hindsight | Anchors back-catalogue + credibility |
| Speculation / theory | Read the tea leaves on the *next* game | High engagement; invites comment debate |

## Tone
Calm, articulate, warm, journalistic — fact-led but openly passionate, cozy not shouty. The analyst you
put on with a cup of tea. Pacing is slow and deliberate; B-roll often a single held frame under VO.
Honesty is a brand asset ("I'd rather give you the honest version than the one that gets clicks").

## The recurring scenarios (repeatable formats)
1. "Breaking down [the reveal]" — frame-by-frame decode of a trailer/announcement.
2. "Everything we know about [the next game]" — consolidated state-of-play reference.
3. The lore explainer — "Who is X, really?" / "The history of Y, explained."
4. The retrospective / post-mortem — "What went wrong with [game]."
5. Industry-watch — layoffs, unionization, cancellations, through a fan-but-informed lens.

## The hook formula (first 30–60s)
1. **Cold-open provocation** — a bold thesis/question stated flat.
2. **Authority + stakes** — "I've covered every reveal for years; this one is different."
3. **Promise the payoff** — "by the end you'll see the whole picture."
4. **Cozy invitation** — "grab a cup of tea, get comfortable, and let's get into it."
5. **Roadmap** — name the segments so the viewer commits to the runtime.

## Title style
Smart curiosity-gap questions: "Could this be the best Dragon Age story in the series?",
"The Next Mass Effect Just Showed Its Hand (And Nobody Noticed)".
